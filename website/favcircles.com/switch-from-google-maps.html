From Takeout to your new map

1. Export your saved places from Google

  1. Go to takeout.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click Deselect all, then select only Saved.
  3. Create the export and download the zip when Google emails you the link.

2. Import into FavCircles

  1. In FavCircles, go to Settings → Data → Import Places.
  2. Pick your Takeout file.
  3. Done. Everything lands in one circle on your map, and your Google lists — "Want to go," "Favorites" — become filterable tags.

Places you've already saved are skipped automatically, so re-importing is safe. Places without coordinates in the export are located automatically after import — their pins appear as you use the app.
