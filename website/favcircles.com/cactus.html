Rewards that are actually real.

Most apps hand out points that die inside the app. FavCoins are a real cryptocurrency on the 🌵 Cactus blockchain — you earn them by living your life, and when you're ready, they leave the app with you.

Start filling your piggy bank
🐷

1. Fill the Piggy Bank

Saving places, likes, comments, follows, shares — everything you do drops coins (and fractions of coins) into your in-app piggy bank. Tap the ($) at Home to watch it grow.

🌵

2. Claim to the Blockchain

When you've got whole coins, claim them. They're minted to your own wallet on the Cactus blockchain — publicly verifiable, permanently yours.

💎

3. Actually Own Them

Once claimed, your FavCoins don't depend on us. Your wallet, your keys, your coins — that's the whole point of crypto.

What earns FavCoins?

  • Building your world: saving places and creating circles
  • Being a good neighbor: likes, comments, reactions, and follows — given and received
  • Spreading the word: sharing places and inviting friends; when your share leads to a save, you earn
  • Showing up: check-ins and store reward redemptions

Small actions earn fractions of a coin; meaningful contributions earn full coins. It adds up faster than you'd think.

What is the Cactus blockchain?

Cactus (CAC) is a community-run, energy-efficient blockchain built on the same proof-of-space-and-time technology as Chia — securing the network with storage instead of power-hungry mining. FavCircles mints FavCoin rewards on Cactus, so every claimed coin is a real on-chain asset you can look up in a public explorer.

Do I need to understand crypto? No. The piggy bank handles everything. Crypto-curious users can claim coins to their own wallet; everyone else can just enjoy watching the balance grow until they're ready.
