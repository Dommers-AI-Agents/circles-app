FavCircles in Claude: walkthrough

Try FavCircles the way a new customer or store owner would, by chatting with Claude. About 15 minutes.

1. Connect Claude (once)

  1. In Claude (claude.ai, the desktop app or the phone app): Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name FavCircles, URL https://mcp.favcircles.com/mcp, then Connect.
  3. On the FavCircles page, sign in with one of the test accounts below, or tap Create an account to try sign-up yourself.

Test accounts (hidden from everyone, can't be found or followed): testingAI and testingAI Store. Their emails and passwords are on your Mac in ~/.config/favcircles-test-accounts.txt. To switch accounts, remove the connector in Claude and add it again.

Heads-up: a brand-new account automatically follows you, your dad and Brittany (same as the app), so creating one sends you a New Follower notification.

2. Before connecting: "what is this?"

These work even without an account:

What is FavCircles? How do I get started with FavCircles? What widgets does FavCircles have? Why should my coffee shop join FavCircles?

Check: the store answer says 3 months free, then $49.99/month.

3. As a new user (testingAI)

Who am I signed in as on FavCircles? Make me a private circle called Date Night. Find a few good cocktail bars in South End Charlotte and add two of them to Date Night with a note. Show me my Date Night circle. Open it on my map.

Check: each place and the circle come back with a link. Tap one on your iPhone: it should open in FavCircles. Notes are private ("only you see it").

Already there: a private "Charlotte coffee" circle with 4 places, from the first test run.

4. As a store owner (testingAI Store)

Do I have any stores on FavCircles? Set up my store. What's left to do? Write a two-sentence description for my café and add it. Show my store dashboard. Post a special: $2 off lattes before 10am.

Check: the checklist counts up as steps get done. The special should come back with the FavCircles Business upgrade offer (this test store is on the free plan).

The store is a fake, hidden test café: "testingAI Test Café (TEST - do not approve)". It isn't in search, the map or Locals.

Claiming a new store

Claim my store: <name, street address, city>

Only try this with a clearly fake business: a claim notifies you for review, just like the app. Don't approve a fake claim against a real business.
