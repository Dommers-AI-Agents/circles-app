Family & friends

🎉

Events

Party bus, trip or night out: photos only your group sees.

  • Start an event and drop one link in the group text, or invite friends in the app
  • A shared photo album only the people who joined can see, from the camera or your photo library
  • Download every photo of the night to your Photos in one tap
  • Tag the places you went and save them to a circle named after the event
  • Roll call ("everyone here?") and photo challenges to keep the night fun
  • A FavCoin 🌵 for joining
💬

How Are You?

Check on Mom or Dad a few times a day.

  • Questions at the times you pick; they answer with one tap from the Lock Screen
  • Questions fit their life: pain from 0 to 10, meds, sleep, PT days, rides
  • A heads-up when an answer needs attention, or when they go quiet
  • Brothers and sisters can join the same check-in
  • Send love on any answer, and say "I'll call" so the family knows it's handled
  • Your parent sees their own week, trends and streaks too
📮

Postcard

A real postcard from your trip photo.

  • Pick a photo and a design, add a note
  • Send it to a friend's chat, by email, or as a link anyone can open
  • Or have it printed and mailed anywhere in the US, with delivery updates
🎨

Fridge Mail

Kids' drawings, mailed to Grandma every week.

  • Snap the drawing; we print it and mail it
  • Send to up to three grandparents on a weekly schedule
🧾

Bill Split

Split the check and the tip in seconds.

  • Enter the bill, pick the tip, see what everyone owes

Going out

🍸

NextBar

Your next bar, picked from where your friends actually go.

  • Picks a bar near you from your saved places and your friends'
  • Start a group vote and let everyone pick where to go next
🍜

What to Eat

Can't decide? One tap picks for you.

  • Spin for a craving and see where to get it nearby, starting with places you and your friends saved
🍹

Make Me a Drink

A cocktail to order tonight, and how to make any classic.

  • Recipes for every spirit, with a surprise-me shake
  • Calories on every drink
  • Send a recipe to a friend's chat

Health & fitness

🏋️

Workouts

Log sets, reps and personal records.

  • Routines that remember last time's numbers
  • Cardio with calories, a rest timer and exercise photos
  • Share a workout with friends; they can copy it as their own routine
🏃

FavRun

Track your runs on a map.

  • GPS route map, pace, splits and personal bests
  • Your run on the Lock Screen while you go
  • Share your runs with the friends you choose
📣

Coach Mane

A coach who won't let you skip.

  • An animated coach with a fresh line every time: tough love, or savage if you dare
  • He'll remind you through the day; tap Did it to keep your streak
  • Send his line to a friend who needs to hear it
💧

Water

One tap per glass.

  • A daily goal, streaks and a month at a glance
  • Reminders every 1 to 4 hours, and log a cup right from the notification
✅

Habits

Daily check-ins and streaks.

  • One-tap check-ins for the habits you want to keep
🥗

Calories

Quick-add meals and macros.

  • Log what you eat in a couple of taps
❤️

Heartbeat

Your pulse, from the camera or a strap.

  • Measure your heart rate with your phone's camera, or connect a Bluetooth heart-rate strap

Every day

Weather

Right now, the next hours and 10 days.

  • The weather where you are, hour by hour and 10 days out
📈

Stocks

Indexes, rates, crypto and the stocks you follow.

  • The Nasdaq, Dow and S&P at a glance, plus your own watchlist
💭

Quotes

Good lines at the times you pick.

  • Pick your topics and how many times a day
  • Share a quote with a friend
🌙

Sleep Sounds

Rain, ocean, fire: mix and drift off.

  • Mix sounds into your own blend
  • A timer that fades out gently, with controls on the Lock Screen
