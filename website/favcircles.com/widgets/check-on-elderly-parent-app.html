Check on Mom or Dad, a few taps a day

How Are You? is a daily check-in app for an aging parent. Pick the questions and the times, and your parent answers with one tap from the Lock Screen. You get a heads-up when an answer needs attention or when they go quiet, and brothers and sisters can share the same check-in.

Get FavCircles, free on iPhone

What How Are You? does

  • Questions at the times you pick; they answer with one tap from the Lock Screen
  • Questions fit their life: pain from 0 to 10, meds, sleep, PT days, rides
  • A heads-up when an answer needs attention, or when they go quiet
  • Brothers and sisters can join the same check-in
  • Send love on any answer, and say "I'll call" so the family knows it's handled
  • Your parent sees their own week, trends and streaks too

Questions

What kinds of questions can I ask?

Questions that fit their life: pain from 0 to 10, whether they took their meds, how they slept, PT days and rides.

Can my siblings see the answers too?

Yes. Brothers and sisters can join the same check-in, and anyone can tap "I'll call" so the family knows it's handled.
