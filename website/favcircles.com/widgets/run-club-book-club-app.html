Run your club from one link

Clubs in FavCircles is an app for run clubs, walk groups, book clubs and any group that meets. Share one link to bring people in, set weekly meetups like "Saturdays 7 AM at the park", see who's going, and keep everyone posted with announcements and a message board.

Get FavCircles, free on iPhone

What Clubs does

  • Start a club (run, walk, ride, hike, swim, yoga, books or anything) and share one link to bring people in
  • Weekly meetups like "Saturdays 7 AM at the park", plus one-off events
  • Tap Going or Can't make it, see who's coming, and get a reminder an hour before
  • A bulletin for organizers' announcements, sent to every member as a notification
  • A message board where anyone can post, react and reply
  • Organizers and members: add co-organizers, remove people, close joining or reset the link
  • Mute a club anytime
  • A FavCoin 🌵 for joining

Questions

What kinds of clubs can I start?

Run, walk, ride, hike, swim, yoga, books or anything else that meets.

Do members get reminders?

Yes. Members who tap Going get a reminder an hour before each meetup, and organizers' announcements arrive as notifications.

Part of FavCircles

Clubs is one of 27 widgets in FavCircles, the free app for saving and sharing your favorite places. Keep the restaurants, coffee shops and spots you love in circles, see where your friends go, and earn rewards at local shops. The widgets live in the app's Widgets tab: turn on the ones you want.
