Plan the party, track the RSVPs, share one album

Events in FavCircles is an RSVP and party planner for iPhone. Set the date and place, invite friends by link or email (they don't need the app to answer), see who's coming with head counts, and keep every photo and video from the night in one album only your guests can see.

Get FavCircles, free on iPhone

What Events does

  • Set the date, time, place and details, and drop one link in the group text, or invite friends in the app
  • Invite anyone by email, no app needed: they tap Going, Maybe or Can't go, say how many adults and kids, and leave you a note
  • See every reply and head count in one guest list, get a notification as people answer, resend an invite, and message your guests
  • Add to calendar, directions, and a reminder the day before for everyone coming
  • A shared album of photos and videos only the people who joined can see, from the camera or your photo library
  • Video clips up to 15 seconds free (5 per event), or up to a minute with Premium
  • Download every photo and video of the night to your Photos in one tap, zoom in on any photo, and save or share a single clip
  • Tap anyone in the event to see their profile
  • Tag the places you went and save them to a circle named after the event
  • Roll call ("everyone here?") and photo challenges to keep the night fun
  • A FavCoin 🌵 for joining

Questions

Do my guests need the app to RSVP?

No. Invite anyone by email and they tap Going, Maybe or Can't go from the invite, with how many adults and kids are coming.

Can everyone add photos to the album?

Yes. Everyone who joined the event can add photos and videos, and download the whole night to their Photos in one tap.
