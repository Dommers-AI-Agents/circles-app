How well you slept, scored every morning

Sleep Score reads your sleep from Apple Health (from your Apple Watch or any sleep app) and scores each night out of 100, from how long you slept, your time asleep in bed, consistency and sleep stages. No watch? Log a night by hand.

Get FavCircles, free on iPhone

What Sleep Score does

  • A nightly score out of 100 from how long you slept, time asleep in bed, consistency and sleep stages
  • Reads your sleep from Apple Health (your Apple Watch or any sleep app), or log a night by hand
  • Your recent nights at a glance, with 7-night averages
  • Private: your Health data stays on your phone

Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. It reads any sleep app that saves to Apple Health, or you can log a night by hand.

Where is my sleep data kept?

Your Health data stays on your phone.
