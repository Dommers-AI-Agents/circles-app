🌵 FavCoins: rewards that are actually real

Most apps hand out points that die inside the app. FavCoins are different — they're a real cryptocurrency on the Cactus blockchain. Being an active part of the community fills your in-app piggy bank, and when you're ready you can send your coins to your own wallet on-chain.

  • Earn for saving places, likes, comments, follows, and sharing FavCircles
  • Earn per-store reward points at participating local businesses
  • Claim your balance to a real blockchain wallet whenever you want
