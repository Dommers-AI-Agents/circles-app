How store rewards work

1

Save & follow your favorites

Add the shops you love to your circles and follow the ones that run rewards.

2

Earn points per shop

Each participating store keeps its own points bucket for you — visiting, sharing, and bringing friends all count.

3

Redeem at the register

Enter a redemption code at the shop or show your reward — real perks at real places, not coupons for stuff you don't want.

The Specials feed

The home screen's Specials tab shows live offers and announcements from shops near you and shops you follow. Store owners post them; you claim them. It's that direct.

  • Limited-time offers from your favorite local spots
  • Announcements when something new is happening
  • Rewards for sharing — when a friend saves a place you shared, you both win

And then there's the crypto 🌵

Store points are per-shop. FavCoins are yours everywhere — a real cryptocurrency you earn across the whole app and can claim to your own wallet on the Cactus blockchain.
