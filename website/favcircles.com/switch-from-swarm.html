Been checking in since Foursquare? Keep going.

If you've spent years logging where you've been, you know the worry: what happens to all of it? FavCircles is a place where checking in still matters — badges and points included — and where the friends who see your check-ins are the ones you actually know.

Start your next chapter of check-ins here

FavCircles gives your check-in habit a home: check in at places you visit, earn streak-style badges and points as your saved-place count grows, and build a real map of everywhere you go. Your finds aren't shouted into a void — friends and connections see them, follow your places, and recommend theirs back.

About your Swarm history: direct Swarm import isn't available in FavCircles yet — it's on our roadmap, and we won't pretend otherwise. What you can do today: request your data export from your Swarm account settings so your check-in history is safely in your hands and ready the moment import lands. In the meantime, start your new check-ins here so there's no gap in the story.

FavCircles does import places from Mapstr and Google Maps today — see switching from Mapstr or importing Google Maps places.
