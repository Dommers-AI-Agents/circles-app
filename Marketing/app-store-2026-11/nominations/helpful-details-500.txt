US launch starts in Charlotte, NC. We are signing up local shops to put a FavCircles sticker in the window and a card at the register. A passerby scans or taps: an App Clip opens, no download, and the store is saved to their map. Visits earn points at that store, kept in Apple Wallet, and friends see the spot on their map. Word of mouth, made visible. Built on App Clips, Wallet, NFC, Live Activities and Apple Maps by a small independent team.
