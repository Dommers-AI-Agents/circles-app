Favvy · counter figure concepts

A Favvy that looks like it belongs by the register

These take the Furbo Mini's shape (a soft cup on a short stem, with a flat lid on top) and make Favvy the character inside it. Each one has a clear spot to tap or scan. The QR is a sticker label, and the NFC tag sits right under it.

The brief: friendly and inviting, elegant and classy, but still fun and a little curious, so people want to touch it. Each card says where it lands on that.

All six are about 11 cm tall, about as tall as a coffee cup, with an 8.5 cm foot weighted with washers so a phone tap won't knock it over.

How they're built

Three printed parts and two stickers

  1. Lid in Bambu's wood-fill PLA (or white), printed flat. A shallow round recess on top holds the 38 mm NFC sticker with the 1.5 in QR label over it, so the tag sits right at the surface.
  2. Body printed upside down on its flat top. The walls lean inward as they rise, so it needs no supports and has no color swaps.
  3. Foot printed flat, with two 1 in steel washers pressed in underneath. The stem keys into the body with a twist-lock peg plus a drop of glue, so it's one solid piece.
  4. Face is a die-cut vinyl sticker: the dark heart window with Favvy's eyes and smile. It looks crisp and needs no multi-color printing.

The three parts print in one color each, about 3 hours in total (an estimate until we slice them). That's far faster than the 12-hour multi-color print.

My pick

Phone Rest, with the Furbo Favvy face

It's the best balance of the brief: elegant enough for any counter, friendly because of the heart-window face, and curious enough that people want to try it. Its lid tilts toward the customer like a little tray, so people lay their phone on it without being told. That's the most reliable tap of the six, because the phone lands flat on the tag every time. It keeps the Furbo silhouette and the friendly heart-window face. Second choice: Furbo Favvy, if you want the cleanest look with a flat lid.
