Step 5 · Loyalty at the counter

The register QR card

  • Keep the register QR card at your counter
  • After a purchase, customers scan it and earn the points you set — limited to once per day per customer
  • Points stay with your shop: earned at your store, spendable only on your store's offers
  • Rotate the card any time — the old printed code stops working immediately
