# FavCircles T-Shirt — "Every great place has a story."

Designed to match the FavCircles coaster and business cards. Same palette, same pin, same voice.

## The idea

The front is the coaster's best line, set inside a **dashed circle with pins dropped on it** — the route
you take through your favorite places, and a literal picture of the word *circle*. The period at the end of
"has a story" is a blue map pin with a ripple under it. No app-store badge, no URL, no logo soup on the
front: it reads as a good tee first and a brand second, which is what makes people actually wear it.

The back is the pitch — a winding dashed route with four pin stations (SAVE / ORGANIZE / SHARE /
REMEMBER) leading down to a QR code that opens the App Store listing. Anyone standing behind you in a
coffee line can scan it.

whatever the shirt color is shows through, so these drop onto black without a visible print box.

## Colors (identical to the coaster / cards)

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | `#3A82C4` | Primary pin, "SAVE", the period-pin |
| Green | `#8DC16D` | "ORGANIZE", accent pins |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Headline, wordmark, "SHARE" |
| Cool gray | `#78848F` | Route line, "REMEMBER", secondary pins |
| Shirt | `#1A1A1C` | Black tee |

**4 spot colors total.** That's deliberate — this can be screen printed as a 4-color job (white, blue,
green, gray) on black, which gets your unit cost way down at 50+ pieces vs. DTG.

## Production notes

- **DTG / print-on-demand** (Printful, Printify): upload as-is. Ask for a white underbase on the blue and
  green so they don't go muddy on black cotton.
- **Screen printing**: 4 screens + white underbase. Ask for a *soft-hand* or water-based/discharge ink —
  a plastisol slab this size will feel like a placemat and people won't wear it. Discharge on 100% ring-spun
  cotton is the version that gets worn.
- **Blank recommendation**: Bella+Canvas 3001 or Comfort Colors 1717 in black. The Comfort Colors garment-dye
  reads more "local shop" and less "conference swag," which suits this design.
- **QR**: uses error-correction level H with the pin knocked into the center. Verified to decode down to
  ~20% of the file's size, so it still scans if the printer scales the back print smaller. It points to
  `https://apps.apple.com/app/id6746807095`.
- **Always order one sample** and scan the QR off the actual fabric before running a batch.
