Setting up a new FavCircles store

Checklist · tick as you go

1Before the visit · in the app, 5 min

  • Find the store in the app and open its page → ••• → Store Admin. If it isn't a FavCircles store yet, this makes it one, with no owner, so the business can still claim it.
  • Print the window sign and a table tent: tap the window code → Share → Print a sign or tent… (print at Actual size).
  • Optional Totem for the counter: Store Admin → NFC stickers → + → Points, hold the phone to the heart. Put the QR label on the base.
  • Bring the starter kit: window sticker, register card, table tents.

2At the store · the 2-minute demo

  • Owner scans the window code with their iPhone camera → their store's card opens (no app needed) → Open → sign in → the store is saved.
  • Show what customers get: their own map of places they love, friends' maps, no strangers' reviews; regulars earn points and come back.
  • Show what the owner gets: loyalty, local reach, online menu and pickup ordering, all run from the phone.
  • Leave the starter kit. The window code is free for every store.

3The owner claims it · same day

  • Owner installs FavCircles, opens their store's page and taps Claim your store.
  • You approve: Settings → Sticker Venues → Claims → Approve. Approval gets the store's codes ready.
  • Add their contact name and email and any managers (Manage Store).

4The owner sets up · Manage Store on the store's page

  • Store details, Opening hours, Photos (choose the cover).
  • Scan your menu: photograph it or pick a PDF; every item goes on the page.
  • Get Business: 3 months free, then $49.99/mo. Turns on rewards and ordering.
  • Points per purchase and Points for saving your store: the owner chooses.
  • Optional: Online ordering (pickup), Bring them back, Bring a friend.
  • Watch the Store owner guide (4-minute video).

5Put the codes out

  • Window sticker on the door or window: scan to save the store.
  • Register card or Totem at the counter: scan or tap for points, once a day, only at the store.
  • Table tents and window-kind NFC stickers on tables.
  • Do a test scan at the register with your phone.

6Follow up

  • Same day: send the store follow-up email with their store card (ask Claude: "send the follow-up for <store>").
  • After a week: Store Admin for saves, visits and scans; Manage Store → Purchases & redemptions.
  • Not on Business after the visit? Offer a 20-minute demo of Manage Store.

What to say (and not say)

  • Points: "you choose how many". Never quote a maximum or a default.
  • One plan: Business, $49.99/mo after 3 free months.
  • Ordering = online menu + pickup. Never delivery.
  • No texting customers: it isn't offered.
  • Rewards are small giveaways; scans need sign-in and location.
  • Only promise features that exist today.
